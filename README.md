## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **METAL GEAR SOLID 4: Guns of the Patriots - Master Collection Version**. This trainer provides optional single-player enhancements for Solid Snake's stealth missions, firefights, equipment management, and survival systems. It supports the game's OctoCamo, Psyche, Grip, ammunition, Drebin Points, alert, and weapon mechanics.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to replay the campaign with reduced difficulty, experiment with weapons and OctoCamo, explore every act without resource limits, or create customized stealth and combat challenges.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Unlimited Health** — Prevents Snake's health from decreasing during gameplay.
- **Unlimited Psyche** — Keeps the Psyche Gauge filled to reduce the effects of stress, fatigue, and battlefield pressure.
- **Unlimited Grip** — Prevents the Grip Gauge from being depleted while Snake hangs from ledges and other surfaces.
- **Stealth Mode** — Makes Snake difficult or impossible for enemies to detect during infiltration.
- **Freeze Alert Status** — Prevents the current alert state from escalating while preserving normal movement and combat controls.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **Unlimited Ammo** — Provides unlimited ammunition for compatible firearms and weapons.
- **No Reload** — Removes the need to reload equipped firearms.
- **One-Hit Kills** — Allows compatible attacks and weapons to defeat enemies with a single successful hit.

### 🧰 Inventory

- **Unlimited Grenades and Items** — Prevents throwable weapons, rations, medicine, and other consumable equipment from being depleted.
- **Max Drebin Points** — Adds a large supply of Drebin Points for purchasing weapons, ammunition, suppressors, and weapon upgrades from Drebin's Shop.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Activate movement-related options after loading into a mission. Unlimited Grip is useful when hanging from ledges or traversing elevated areas. Stealth Mode is best used during infiltration and can be disabled before scripted encounters or transitions.

### Tools & Inventory

Use Unlimited Ammo and Unlimited Grenades and Items to experiment with Snake's large selection of firearms, explosives, rations, medicine, and support equipment. Max Drebin Points makes it easier to purchase and customize weapons through Drebin's Shop.

### World & Environment

The campaign moves through several acts and active battlefields where Snake can support local forces, avoid patrols, use cover, and take advantage of the OctoCamo system. Freeze Alert Status can help preserve exploration by preventing normal alert escalation.

### Combat & Weapons

Unlimited Health and Psyche improve survivability during extended firefights, while No Reload and Unlimited Ammo support continuous fire. One-Hit Kills can shorten encounters, but disabling it is recommended for boss fights and scripted battles where normal combat behavior is preferred.
